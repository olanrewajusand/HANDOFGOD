NHF Requirements

Use these checklists to prepare for an NHF-supported application.

Formal Sector

  1. Five passport photographs
  2. BVN print out
  3. Valid ID card with NIN number
  4. NHF passbook
  5. Six months' recent salary-account bank statement
  6. Three months' recent payslip
  7. Letter of appointment and confirmation
  8. Evidence of additional income, if any
  9. One next-of-kin passport

Informal Sector

  1. Five passport photographs
  2. BVN print out
  3. Valid ID card with NIN number
  4. Six months' recent bank statement
  5. Evidence of additional income, if any
  6. One next-of-kin passport

Requirements may change. Confirm the current checklist with our customer service team before submitting documents.
