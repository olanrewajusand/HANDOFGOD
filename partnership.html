The partnership

Why this partnership matters.

This project originates from the need to develop the southeast region.

The development of the 100,000 housing units is in partnership with the government of the various states and IGBOEBINIE PLC, which is the umbrella body of Igbos across the world.

In other to achieve this, Hand of God Construction Company Limited is leading other REDAN members who are developers in the southeast. This is viewed as the Igbo movement. The partnership is calling on all

South-easterners especially those in diaspora to collaborate with the Government, REDAN, IGBOEBINIE PLC and HAND OF GOD CONSTRUCTION COMPANY LTD.Learn more about IGBOEBINIE
