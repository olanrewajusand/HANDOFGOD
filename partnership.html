The partnership

Why this partnership matters.

In a region with growing demand for secure housing, industrial corridors, and modern infrastructure, Hand of God Construction Company Limited and IGBOEBINIE PLC are combining capital, project execution, and a community-first development model to deliver a transformational urban vision.

With the support of strategic stakeholders and a clear roadmap, the partnership is designed to unlock a major real estate movement across the South East—one that connects quality homes, commercial hubs, and long-term returns.

Shared mission

  • Deliver modern, affordable, and premium homes at scale.
  • Build the infrastructure needed for a thriving regional economy.
  • Create an enduring platform for diaspora and local investment.
