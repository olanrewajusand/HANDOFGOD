Our Profile

A committed real estate development firm

HAND OF GOD CONSTRUCTION COMPANY LIMITEDis a duly registered real estate development firm in Nigeria and a committed member of the Real Estate Developers Association of Nigeria (REDAN). HAND OF GOD CONSTRUCTION COMPANY LIMITEDis a consortium of professionals with over Ten (10) years' experience in construction industry, with keen drive to deliver projects on real estate, constructions, qualitative human capital development, provide leadership to enhance project managements etc.

Studies show that about 65% of mid income earners in Nigeria do not have access to decent housing of their own thereby lacking basic necessity for comfortable living.

Given the housing challenges, HAND OF GOD CONSTRUCTION COMPANY LIMITEDin collaboration with her subsidiary Hand of God Investment Cooperative Society Limited and Iconnect has established a workable frame work to address housing problem by providing affordable housing for mid-income earners in Nigeria.

Our willingness to constantly innovate and deliver within contract terms and without the constraint of resources has enabled us to conclude some projects around the South East of Nigeria to the satisfaction of our clientele.
