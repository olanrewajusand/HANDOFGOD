About HAND OF GOD CONSTRUCTION COMPANY

A practical path to better home ownership.

HAND OF GOD CONSTRUCTION COMPANY LIMITEDis a registered Nigerian real estate development firm and a member of REDAN. Our consortium brings more than ten years of construction experience to affordable housing, estate development, and project delivery.

Together with Hand of God Investment Cooperative Society Limited, we create convenient routes to ownership for mid-income earners through quality homes, planned communities, and flexible payment options.
