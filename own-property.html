What You Need

Documentation & Requirements

Simple, straightforward requirements to get you started on your property ownership journey.

👤

Personal Documents

  • ☑

    Valid Government ID

    National ID, Driver's License, or Passport

  • ☑

    Proof of Residence

    Recent utility bill or letter from landlord

  • ☑

    Proof of Income

    Recent pay slips or bank statements (3 months)

  • ☑

    NHF Contribution Status (if applicable)

    NHF balance or contribution history

💳

Financial Documents

  • ☑

    Bank Statements

    Last 3-6 months to verify financial stability

  • ☑

    Tax Returns

    Last year's tax declaration (if self-employed)

  • ☑

    Employment Letter

    From your current employer confirming position

  • ☑

    Deposit/Initial Payment

    As per your chosen payment plan

ℹ️

Need Assistance?

Don't worry if you're unsure about any requirements. Our dedicated team is here to guide you through the entire process. We'll explain each requirement and help you gather the necessary documents.
